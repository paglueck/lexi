# Project governance

Lexi was initiated by Patrick Glück, who currently maintains the canonical project at https://github.com/paglueck/lexi and makes roadmap and release decisions.

Lexi is free software under the GNU General Public License v3.0 or later. Contributions come in under the same license (inbound = outbound) and are certified with a Developer Certificate of Origin sign-off; see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md). There is no contributor license agreement, so every contributor keeps the copyright in their work, and the project as a whole can only be distributed under GPL-compatible terms.

Contributions are reviewed for usefulness, maintainability, provenance and licensing. Contributors are credited accurately. A contribution does not grant ownership of the Lexi project or decision-making authority over its roadmap.

External changes use pull requests. The maintainer may merge their own PR after required checks; external PRs receive maintainer review. Changes to licensing, security policy and the contribution process require explicit documented review. Future maintainers and succession arrangements will be documented here.

Support is best effort unless separately contracted.
